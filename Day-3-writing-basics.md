## 2. Key Takeaways 

* **Core Goals:** Enable readers to easily **find**, **understand**, and **use** a document.
* **Clarity & Conciseness:** Keep writing brief and clear for specific readers.
  * **Cut filler:** Use *"Now"* instead of *"At this point in time.*" 
  * **Strong verbs:** Choose specific, direct action verbs.
* **Sentence Building Blocks:** Use **Subject + Verb + Object** (e.g., *The server [subject] sends [verb] the receiver [object]*).
* **Common Sentence Errors:** 
  * **Clause structure:** Connect dependent and main clauses properly (e.g., *"When the file is saved, a message appears "*).
  * **Subject-verb agreement:** Match singular/ plural forms (e.g., *"The files **are** saved not *"The files **is** saved"*).
* **Punctuation & Consistency:** Master daily punctuation-period (`.`), comma (`,`), semi colon (`;`), colon (`:`), apostrophe (`'`), and em dash (`-`)-and apply formatting consistently.
* **Active vs. Passive voice:**
  * **Active (Preferred):** Focuses on the **actor** doing the action.
  * **Passive:** Focuses on the **object** when the actor is omitted or secondary.
* **Tone:** Sound like a **knowledgeable friend**-conversational, friendly, and respectful (neither too formal nor too informal).
