# Webinar notes: From Curious To Certified: What It Really Takes 

- **Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Organized By:** Tech Crush
- **Speakers:** Abiodun Ayinde & Abdul Raheem
- 
- ## 1. Key Takeaways & Mindset
- **Start with what you have:** Don't wait for ideal gadgets; begin where you are and optimize overtime.
- **Master the basics:** Core fundamentals are your anchor at every stage of tech.
- **Avoid skill hopping:** Build deep competence in one skill before moving to the next.

- ## 2. Practical Experience & Execution
- **Active learning:** Move beyond theory by joining team projects and volunteering.
- **Tool proficiency:** Know how to use your tools effectively to solve problems.
- **Embrace challenges:** Don't shy away from big tasks-push your boundaries and assess what you need to grow.

- ## 3. Networking & Visibility
-  **Join communities:** Surround yourself with peers and mentors.
-  **Public presence:** Showcase your skills across social platforms ( LinkedIn, Twitter, e.t.c) to unlock referrals & opportunities.
