## 4. How The Bootcamp Works 
* **Live Classes:** Two interactive Zoom sessions every week from **7:30 p.m - 9:00 p.m**.
* **Weekly Assignments:** Short writing tasks designed to reinforce each week 's lessons.
* **Mini Projects:** Real-world scenarios to practice technical writing skills.
* **Peer Feedback:** Reviewing eachother's draft to learn and grow together.
* **WhatsApp Community:** Stay active for class announcements, asking questions, and peer support between sessions.
* **Capstone Project (weeks 9-12):** A final project where each learner builds a complete technical document from scratch.

* ---

## 5. What We Need To Succeed
* **Show Up Consistently:** Be on time and stay present-consistency beats talents.
* **Read with Curiosity:** Pay close attention to how user guides, manuals, and help pages are structured in every day life.
* **Finish and Submit on Time:** Always submit assignments by the deadline, even if the draft does not feel perfect yet.
* **Write Often:** Practice writing regularly, even if it is just **10 minutes a day** to sharpen your skills.
* **Ask Questions:** Never hesitate to ask for clarity during live classes or in the WhatsApp community.
* **Give & Welcome Constructive Feedback:** Offer respectful, healthy critiques without insults-remember that feedback is about helping everyone improve.
