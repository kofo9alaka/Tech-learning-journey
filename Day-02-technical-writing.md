## 1. What is Technical Writing?
**Technical Writing** is the practice of explaining technical or specialized information clearly so that a specific reader can understand it and use it effectively.

### Technical Writing Vs. Creative Writing 
| Features | Technical Writing | Creative Writing 
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Purpose** | To help the reader**understand** and use information. | To **entertain** the reader. |
| **Target Audience** | Written for a**specific audience**. | Written for **general audience**. |
| **Outcome** Helps the user complete a **specific task**. | Aims to evoke**emotions**. |

---

## 2. The Role of a Technical Writer
A Technical writer bridges the gap between complex information and the end user through several core responsibilities:

* **Learn User Needs:** Research and discover what users actually need and want to accomplish.
* **Write and Revise:** Create manuals, guides, how-to tutorials, and Q&A resources.
* **Collaborate with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs):** Interview experts to gather accurate information and translate it clearly for those who need it.
* **Edit and Improve:** Review and refine content written by others to elevate clarity and quality.

### 💡 Key Insight: Technical Writing & AI
Even in the age of AI, human technical writers remain indispensable:
* **The Human Brain Behind the Prompt:** AI relies on human researchers to verify facts, check sources, and establish truth before prompting AI to assist with drafting.
* **High Market Value:** Many professionals and companies rely on documentation without having the time or skill to write it to themselves. Employers highly value writers who very facts, structure contents around users needs, maintain technical accuracy, and can read code and API

 ## 3. Types of Technical Writing
During class, we explored seven primary categories of technical documentation and practiced identifying them through a scenario activity (such as helping a new employee troubleshoot a computer/cash payment issue using an SOP):

1. **User Manuals & Guides:** Explain how to setup, configure and use a product from start to finish.
2. **How-To Guides & Tutorials:** Walk readers through step-by-step instructions to complete a specific or difficult task.
3. **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Document routine tasks that must be executed the exact same way every time for consistency and compliance.
4. **Reports:** Present findings, research, observations and data learned during a project or investigation.
5. **API & Developer Documentation:**  Show web developers and engineers how to integrate with and use software tools.
6. **F&Q and knowledge bases:** provide quick, searchable answers to common user questions and troubleshooting issues.
7. **Emails, Memos, & Internal notes:** Record decision and keep cross-functional teams aligned.

 ## 4. Skills Every Technical Writer Needs
 To succeed in this field,a technical writer must cultivate six core skills:
 * **Clear Writing:** The ability to explain specialized information simply and directly.
 * **Critical Thinking:** simplifying complex, abstract ideas so non-experts can grasp them effortlessly.
 * **Attention to Details:** Being thorough and precise about every single step in a process.
 * **Empathy ( "Beginner's eyes"):** Using imagination to view a product or problem from the perspective of a first-time user.
 * **Teamwork:** Collaborating smoothly across diverse teams, include web developers, engineers, designers, and project managers.
 * **Technical Curiosity:** Maintaining a continuous eagerness to learn across a wide variety of evolving topics and technologies.
